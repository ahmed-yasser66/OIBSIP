Quick answers

Popular questions

I forgot my password, what should I do?

Go to the log in page and choose "Forgot your password?". Enter your email or username and we will send you a link to set a new one.

Why was my payment declined?

Check that your card is enabled for online payments and has enough balance. If you prefer, you can switch to Fawry or a mobile wallet from your Account Overview.

How do I change my plan?

Open your Account Overview, find "Your Plan" and tap "Change plan". The new plan starts at your next billing date.

My downloads are not working offline

Make sure you connect to the internet at least once every 30 days so Premium can verify your account, then re-download the playlist.
