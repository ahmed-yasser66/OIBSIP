Got questions?

Frequently asked questions

How does the Spotify Premium trial work?

If you have never subscribed to Premium before, you get your first 30 days entirely free. You will need to submit a payment method at sign up to ensure continuous listening, but you won't be charged until the trial ends. You can cancel online at any moment before day 30 and you won't pay a single piastre.

How do I cancel my Premium subscription?

Canceling is instant and hassle-free directly from your account page online. Go to your Account Overview, locate "Your Plan", and tap "Change plan" or "Cancel Premium". Your Premium benefits will remain active until the end of your ongoing billing cycle.

What payment options are available in Egypt?

Spotify Egypt supports international & local Egyptian debit/credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Meeza). Furthermore, you can purchase prepaid passes without a recurring bank card using Fawry cash payment at outlets nationwide, as well as electronic wallets like Vodafone Cash, Orange Money, and Etisalat Cash.

How does the Duo or Family plan verify members?

To join a Duo or Family plan, members simply confirm that they live at the same home address during setup. Each person retains their own personal account, username, private playlists, and recommendations without sharing credentials.

Can I keep my downloaded music if I cancel?

If your Premium expires or is cancelled, your account automatically reverts to Spotify Free. You will keep all your created playlists, saved songs, and history intact, but offline playback and ad-free streaming will pause until you re-subscribe.
